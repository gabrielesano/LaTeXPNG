# Privacy Policy — LaTeX to PNG (Chrome Extension)

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

**LaTeX to PNG does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user data.**
Everything the extension does happens locally, inside your browser.

## What the extension stores (on your device only)

To make the popup convenient to use, the extension saves the following in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- the formula you are currently editing (draft);
- your last 5 equations (history) and your bookmarked equations;
- your settings: text colour, resolution (DPI), padding and theme.

This data never leaves your device: it is not synced, uploaded or sent to the developer or to any third party. The theme preference is also kept in the popup's `localStorage` so the correct theme can be applied before the popup is drawn.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not make network requests: MathJax, which renders the formulas, is bundled with the extension and everything works offline.
- It does not load or run remote code.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or cookies.
- It does not read the pages you visit, your browsing history or any personal information.

## Permissions

- **storage** — to save the data listed above on your device.
- **downloads** — to save the generated PNG as a file when you click "Download PNG". The extension does not read or modify any other downloads.

Copying an image to the clipboard happens only when you click "Copy Image".

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the extension removes all the data it stored. You can also remove individual items from the history and bookmarks lists inside the popup.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
